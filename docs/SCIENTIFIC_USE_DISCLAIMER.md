# Scientific Use Disclaimer

The web platform provides spatial decision-support products for
Mandya District groundwater recharge analysis.

The AI layers represent spatial prediction of the FAHP-derived
groundwater recharge index.

They should not be interpreted as direct measurements of groundwater
recharge.

Candidate D8 pathway diagnostics are provenance-limited candidate
products. They are not the authoritative Section 8 groundwater-flow
product and do not constitute direct groundwater-flow validation.

The current deployment package does not establish instantaneous
real-time groundwater prediction.

Decision-support products should be interpreted together with the
project's documented assumptions, validation framework, uncertainty
information and provenance records.
